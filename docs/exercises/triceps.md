## Conditions

No triceps exercise belongs to a choice group. The legacy `cable-triceps-pushdown` is a fixed slot on the built-in Push day; the built-in Push day selector (which offers `push-main` alternatives for the main chest slot) does not apply here. All other entries are catalog-only additions available for custom routines.
