## Conditions

No shoulder exercise belongs to a choice group. The two legacy entries (`incline-bench-lateral-raises`, `rear-delt-fly`) are fixed slots on the built-in Push day; the built-in Push day selector (which offers `push-main` alternatives for the main chest slot) does not apply here. All other entries are catalog-only additions available for custom routines.
